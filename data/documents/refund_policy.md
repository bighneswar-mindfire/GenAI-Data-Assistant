# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase for any product
that is unused, unopened, and in its original packaging. Digital products and
gift cards are non-refundable.

## Process
Refund requests must be submitted through the customer support portal with
the original order number. Approved refunds are processed within 5-7
business days and issued to the original payment method.

## Partial Refunds
Products returned with signs of use or missing original packaging may be
eligible for a partial refund of up to 50% of the purchase price, at the
discretion of the support team.

## Exceptions
Custom or personalized orders are non-refundable unless the item arrived
damaged or defective. Damaged or defective items must be reported within 7
days of delivery to qualify for a full refund or replacement.

## Shipping Costs
Original shipping costs are non-refundable. Customers are responsible for
return shipping costs unless the return is due to a company error.
