# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
All full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
credited at a rate of 1.5 days per month. Unused annual leave may be carried
over into the next year up to a maximum of 10 days; any balance above that is
forfeited on December 31st.

## Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical
certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick
leave does not carry over between years.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave must
be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Applying for Leave
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business
days in advance, except for sick leave, which may be reported on the day of
absence. Requests are approved by the employee's direct manager.

## Unpaid Leave
Employees with at least 1 year of tenure may request up to 30 days of unpaid
leave per year, subject to manager and HR approval.
