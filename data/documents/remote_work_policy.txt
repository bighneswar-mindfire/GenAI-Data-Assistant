Remote Work Policy

Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probation period and whose role
does not require regular on-site presence are eligible to work remotely up
to 3 days per week.

Core Hours
Remote employees must be online and reachable between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM
in their local time zone, regardless of work location.

Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office stipend of $300 for
remote employees to set up an ergonomic workstation.

Fully Remote Roles
Certain roles designated as "fully remote" in the job posting are exempt
from the 3-day cap and may work remotely full-time, subject to manager
approval and quarterly check-ins.

Security Requirements
Remote employees must use company-issued VPN software when accessing
internal systems and must not use public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks for
work involving customer data.
