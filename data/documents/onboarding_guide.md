# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Day One
IT provisions a laptop, email account, and access badges one week before the
employee's start date. New hires receive a welcome email with their first-day
schedule and a link to complete tax and banking forms.

## Week One
New employees attend a company orientation covering mission, values, and
benefits enrollment. Each new hire is assigned a "buddy" from their team to
help with day-to-day questions during the first 30 days.

## First 30 Days
Managers hold a 1:1 check-in at the end of week 1, week 2, and week 4 to
review progress against the onboarding plan. Employees complete mandatory
compliance training (security awareness and code of conduct) within the
first 2 weeks.

## Benefits Enrollment
Employees have 30 days from their start date to enroll in health insurance,
retirement contributions, and other optional benefits. Enrollment outside
this window requires a qualifying life event.

## 90-Day Review
At the end of the probation period, managers submit a 90-day performance
review. Successful completion confirms the employee's transition to regular
full-time status, including remote work eligibility.
