# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim reimbursement for pre-approved travel, client meals,
software subscriptions required for work, and conference registration fees.

## Submission
All expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30
days of the expense being incurred, along with an itemized receipt. Claims
submitted after 30 days require manager sign-off and finance approval.

## Meal Limits
Meal expenses while traveling for business are capped at $75 per day. Team
meals and client entertainment require pre-approval from a manager if the
total exceeds $200.

## Travel
Economy class flights are the default for trips under 6 hours. Business
class may be approved for flights longer than 6 hours or for employees
traveling with a medical accommodation.

## Reimbursement Timeline
Approved reimbursements are paid out with the next payroll cycle following
approval, typically within 10 business days.
